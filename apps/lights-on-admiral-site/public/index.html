04 About the wings

Admiral is more than our address.

Our streets carry the names of admiral butterflies—Red Admiral, Blue Admiral, Vice Admiral, and Admiral. That neighborhood detail became the signal behind this show: bold wings, built from light, moving together with the music.

Lights On Admiral is a homegrown show from our family to yours—for everyone who loves LED lights, music, and that unmistakable feeling when the two come alive together.

We create Halloween, Veterans Day, and Christmas shows, along with mockups, layouts, and the occasional just-for-fun display. We love hearing what viewers enjoyed and what they want to see next.
